### Research Areas

<div class="research-grid">

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Deep Biosphere</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Investigating microbial life within the deep, hot, terrestrial subsurface — including carbon and energy metabolism in extreme and oligotrophic environments, acetogenesis, and isolation of extremophilic microorganisms from deep granitic and basaltic systems.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Microbial Genomics &amp; Metagenomics</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Applying next-generation sequencing and metagenomic approaches to characterize microbial community composition, diversity, and functional potential across a range of extreme and contaminated environments.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Geomicrobiology</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Understanding the interactions between microorganisms and geological systems, including microbial roles in arsenic mobilization in Bengal delta aquifers, acid mine drainage, and biogeochemical cycling in subsurface crustal environments.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Bioremediation</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Developing microbial strategies for the remediation of heavy metals, radionuclides, hydrocarbons, and plastics from contaminated environments, including landfill waste, petroleum-impacted soils, and arsenic-contaminated groundwater.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Astrobiology</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Exploring the limits of life in extreme terrestrial environments as analogs for potentially habitable extraterrestrial settings, with a focus on chemolithotrophs and deep subsurface microbial ecosystems.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Microbial CO&#8322; Capture &amp; Utilization</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Investigating the CO&#8322; fixation capabilities of chemoautotrophic microbial communities from deep subsurface rocks for potential application in third-generation biorefineries and sustainable carbon utilization technologies.</p>
</div>
</div>

</div>
